## Ordering and receiving an account

1. Read the individual listing. Delivery may use a phone number and a code-retrieval address, or session, json, or tdata files. Available formats vary by product; every format is not included in every order. SMS receipt is not guaranteed. Consult the [login and delivery-format guide](https://xixipifa.com/articles/telegram-login-delivery-formats-zh) if a format is unfamiliar.
2. After payment, open the original order to find the account details, files, or collection instructions. Check the product and quantity. Keep credentials private and avoid exposing them in public screenshots or repositories.
3. Start recording before the first login and record the complete manual login process. Do not use scripts to log in. Retain the order number and original delivery information.
4. Once logged in, change the two-step verification password and recovery email as soon as the account's available settings allow. Check Premium status and validity. Preserve any error messages and contact website support with the order number and recording if needed.
